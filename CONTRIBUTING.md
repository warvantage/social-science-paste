# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Social Science Paste.

The project currently focuses on text-format cleaning for academic-writing workflows. Bug reports and reproducible compatibility reports are especially useful.

When reporting a compatibility issue, please include:

- macOS version
- application and version
- copy source and paste destination
- which of Bold / Italic / Hyperlinks were enabled
- a minimal example showing the unexpected result

Structured tables and non-text clipboard content are outside the current v1.0 scope.

By contributing code, you agree that your contribution is provided under the repository's MPL-2.0 licence.
