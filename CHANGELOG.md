# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First stable release of Social Science Paste.

- Copy-time clipboard sanitisation
- Independently selectable Bold preservation
- Independently selectable Italic preservation
- Independently selectable Hyperlink preservation
- Removes source font, size, colour, highlight and related presentation styling
- Preserves text content, Unicode and line breaks
- Pause / Resume clipboard cleaning
- Launch at Login
- English and Chinese interface
- Apple Silicon, macOS 11+

## 1.0.1-beta

Beta release with additional Microsoft Word / Office text-clipboard compatibility improvements.

- Includes all v1.0.0 features
- Improved classification of Office text clipboard payloads
- Better handling of auxiliary Office clipboard representations when the copied content is textual
